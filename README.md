## What is on the picture

- Serif date masthead: "ONE DAY OF WORLD NEWS, ONE PICTURE".
- **The Day in UTC** — 7 timed events, and **The Day in Numbers** — 6 big numbers.
- 3 illustrated leads, each with a number banner above a flat illustration.
- 10 illustrated tiles, each a full-colour flat scene (not a thin icon).
- A verification badge on every story: OFFICIAL (primary source), CONFIRMED
  (2+ outlets), REPORTED (single outlet or claim).
- A world map with numbered pins, a dense 3-column index of 22 more stories, and
  a DATE CHECK box listing what was deliberately excluded as earlier news and
  what has not happened yet.

Everything is drawn with PIL shapes only — no photographs, no AI-generated images.
